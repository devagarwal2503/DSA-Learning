2. Copy the root template: `TEMPLATE.cpp` (at repo root) into the matching `solutions/` folder.
3. Solve, annotate with your **Aha! Moment**, and fill in `notes.md` with key concepts.
4. Mark the problem status in `problems.md` from **Lock (locked)** to **Check (solved)**!
